# Product

## Register
product (Operate surface: a daily-use study tool).

## Users and job
English speakers learning Danish vocabulary in short sessions, mostly on a phone (commute, break), sometimes at a desk with a keyboard. Job: hear a frequent Danish word, read it, recall its meaning; then repeat.

## Product truth
Static, dependency-free app on GitHub Pages: quiz (four options, keys 1-4/arrows/WASD) and flip cards over the top 500 Danish lemmas by frequency, with neural audio, IPA, gender (en/et) and inflections. Attribution footer is required (CC BY-SA).

## Brand commitments
Chosen by the agent per the brief ("choose a nice design"), labelled assumptions: the world is Royal Copenhagen blue-fluted porcelain: committed cobalt on white, light by default (daylight, phone), dark follows system. Avoid the default Danish-flag red + cream.

## Constraints
No build step, no runtime dependencies, self-hosted font, works from a subpath (relative URLs), thumb-reachable controls, 44px targets, respects reduced motion.
